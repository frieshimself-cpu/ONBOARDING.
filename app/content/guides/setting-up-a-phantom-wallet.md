# Setting up a Phantom Wallet

Your wallet is your identity and your bank on Solana. This guide walks you from
zero to a funded, secured [Phantom](https://phantom.app) wallet.

> **Golden rule:** No one — not a mod, not "support", not this site — will ever
> need your seed phrase. Anyone who asks for it is trying to rob you.

## 1. Install the extension or app

1. Go to **[phantom.app](https://phantom.app)** and download for your browser
   (Chrome, Brave, Firefox, Edge) or phone (iOS / Android).
2. **Verify the URL.** Only install from `phantom.app` or the official app store
   listing. Fake wallets are the #1 way newcomers lose funds.
3. Pin the extension so you can spot the real one every time.

## 2. Create a new wallet

1. Open Phantom and choose **Create a new wallet**.
2. Set a strong password (this only unlocks the app on *this* device).
3. Phantom shows your **Secret Recovery Phrase** — 12 words.

### Securing your seed phrase

- Write the 12 words on paper, in order. Consider two copies in two locations.
- Do **not** screenshot it, email it, or paste it into any website.
- Whoever holds these words controls the wallet. Full stop.

## 3. Get your address

Your public address (starts with something like `7xKX…aZ1d`) is safe to share —
it's how people send you tokens and tips. Click your account name at the top of
Phantom to copy it.

## 4. Fund it with SOL

You need a little SOL to pay network fees (fractions of a cent per transaction).

- **Buy in-app** with a card via Phantom's on-ramp, **or**
- **Transfer** SOL from an exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, Binance) by pasting your
  Phantom address as the withdrawal destination.

Always send a **tiny test amount first** when using a new address.

## 5. Connect to $ONBOARDING

1. Click **Connect Wallet** in the top-right of this site.
2. Pick **Phantom** and approve the connection.
3. Connecting only shares your public address — it **cannot** move funds. Every
   transaction still requires you to review and approve it in Phantom.

## Staying safe

- Bookmark the sites you use; don't click wallet links from DMs.
- Review every transaction preview before signing — check amounts and recipients.
- Use a separate "burner" wallet for risky mints; keep your main funds elsewhere.
