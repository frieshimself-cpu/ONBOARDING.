# Launching on Pump.fun

[Pump.fun](https://pump.fun) lets anyone launch a Solana token in minutes using a
bonding-curve model. It's fast — which means the *decisions* matter more than the
clicks. This guide covers both.

> **Before you launch:** a token is a public, permanent thing. Have a name, a
> reason, and a plan for who does what. "Utility" and return claims are exactly
> what draws regulatory attention — keep messaging honest and avoid promising
> profits.

## What Pump.fun actually does

- You create a coin with a name, ticker, and image.
- It launches on a **bonding curve**: price rises as people buy, falls as they sell.
- Once the curve fills (a set market cap), liquidity "graduates" to a DEX
  (e.g. Raydium) and trades like a normal token.

## Decide these first

| Decision | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Which wallet launches it** | The launch wallet controls creator rewards. See the [creator-rewards guide](/guides/routing-creator-rewards). |
| **Name / ticker / art** | Permanent and public. Get the mascot from an [artist onboardee](/onboardees). |
| **Initial dev buy** | How much you buy at launch. Large dev buys look predatory; be transparent. |
| **Socials ready?** | An X account and a place to talk (Telegram/Discord) before launch, not after. |

## Step-by-step

1. **Connect** your Phantom wallet to pump.fun (see the
   [Phantom guide](/guides/setting-up-a-phantom-wallet)).
2. Click **Create coin**.
3. Fill in **name**, **ticker**, **description**, and upload your **image**.
4. Add your **social links** (X, Telegram, website).
5. Optionally set an **initial buy** — the amount of your own coin you purchase at
   launch. You can set this to 0.
6. Review the SOL cost (creation + your dev buy + fees) and **confirm in Phantom**.
7. Share your coin page. Trading is live immediately.

## After launch

- **Don't rug.** Dumping your dev bag destroys trust instantly and may be illegal.
- Communicate. Post updates, ship the thing you said you'd ship.
- Track progress toward graduation, but don't fake volume or pay for undisclosed
  shills — disclosure rules apply.
